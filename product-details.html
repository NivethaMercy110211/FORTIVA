Composite FortiCore™ Matrix

The walls and door slab are poured using FORTIVA’s proprietary FortiCore™ composite: a high-density micro-silica matrix reinforced with corundum aggregates, hardened steel needle fibers, and thermal expansion plates. This barrier structure actively blunts high-speed diamond core drills and dissipates oxy-acetylene torch heat.

Glass Plate Thermal Relockers

Positioned directly behind the manganese anti-drill lock case sits an optical tempered glass relocker trigger. If an attacker applies mechanical drill force or torch flame, the glass shatters instantaneously, unleashing independent spring-loaded steel deadlock pins into hardened channels, permanently deadlocking the boltwork even if the electronic lock is destroyed.

Quad-Direction Active Boltwork

Eight massive 40 mm chromium-plated solid steel locking bolts engage horizontally, vertically, and into fixed rear anti-pry dog bolts along the hinge jamb, making hinge removal completely ineffective.

Structural Floor Load Advisory

At 380 kg tare mass, the Sovereign Grade III distributes approximately 840 kg/m² across its base. Ground floor concrete slabs accommodate this effortlessly. For upper timber joist floors, our engineers calculate joist spacing and provide bespoke load-distribution steel underplates during your site survey.

Discreet Delivery & Transit Protocol

Delivery is executed using our unbranded climate-controlled transport vehicles. Technicians wear plain corporate uniforms without safe-company insignia. Heavy transit occurs using motorized stair-crawlers and protective neoprene floor runners that preserve delicate marble, parquet, and limestone surfaces without leaving marks.
