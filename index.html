04 — Technical Detail

Engineered for Confidence

Understanding safe specifications helps you make the right choice. Here's what every key rating means.

Burglary Resistance vs. Fire Resistance

These are two separate standards tested independently. A safe with a high fire rating does not automatically provide burglary resistance — and vice versa. Always check both certifications for complete protection.

Security Rating (EN 1143-1)

European standard for burglary resistance. Grades range from 0 (entry-level) to VI (highest residential grade). Higher grades indicate greater resistance to physical attack, certified by an independent test laboratory.

Fire Resistance Rating (EN 1047-1)

Defines how long paper (120°C interior limit) or data (70°C interior limit) is protected during a fire test at a specified external temperature. Common ratings: 30, 60, 90 and 120 minutes. Not the same as burglary grade.

Lock Type

Electronic (digital) locks offer quick access and audit trails. Mechanical combination locks require no batteries and never lock you out due to power failure. Many safes offer dual-lock with an emergency key override.
