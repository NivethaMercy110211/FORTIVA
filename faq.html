Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common homeowner questions regarding safe selection, insurance Eurogrades, floor structural loads, discreet delivery, and lock access.

Underwriters typically calculate your policy requirement based on your total insured values. In the UK, high-value items like watches and jewelry receive 10 times the overnight cash cover. For example, a Eurograde 3 safe with a £35,000 cash rating covers up to £350,000 in jewelry. We provide written technical compliance documentation directly to your insurance broker to ensure pre-approval.

Mass-market retail safes are constructed from thin 1.5mm to 2mm sheet metal with hollow doors and basic solenoid locks that can be defeated within 60 to 90 seconds using basic hand tools or heavy magnets. True Eurograde safes undergo accredited testing (EN 1143-1) where independent labs attack the safe with thermal torches, heavy core drills, and carbide blades.

Discretion is fundamental to our service. Our logistics fleet is completely unbranded with zero company logos or signage. Technicians arrive in discreet corporate attire. The safe is transported inside heavy protective crates resembling high-end furniture or AV equipment. All personnel operate under signed non-disclosure agreements.

Yes, when properly engineered. While ground-floor concrete slabs provide immediate high load capacity, upper floors with timber joists require our structural load assessment. We calculate joist spans and install custom 10mm laser-cut steel distribution plates across multiple joists with through-bolts, spreading the load safely within building regulation deflection limits.

We use high-resolution thermal imaging cameras to map warm water pipes or electric heating cables within the screed. If drilling into the floor poses any risk, we utilize heavy rear-wall chemical anchoring into structural masonry or install a certified structural steel bonding plate that adheres chemically to the floor slab without puncturing heating conduits.

Your programmed master codes and biometric enrollments are saved permanently in non-volatile memory and will never be lost. Every FORTIVA electronic safe features external emergency battery contact terminals on the face of the keypad. Simply holding a standard 9V battery against these terminals powers the lock instantly so you can enter your code and change the internal cells.
