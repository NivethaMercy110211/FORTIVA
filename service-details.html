Total Perimeter Defense

Engineered from Modular FortiPanels™

Our modular vault panel system allows high-security walk-in rooms to be erected inside existing basements, master dressing suites, or newly designed residential wings without structural demolition.

Modular Interlocking Panels

Interlocking 100 mm to 200 mm composite panels manufactured with micro-reinforced fiber concrete, corundum carbide, and continuous internal anti-drill mesh. Tested to EN 1143-1 Vault Standards (Class 1 to Class 5).

Certified Vault Doors

Precision-hung steel vault doors weighing up to 900 kg engineered with quad-directional locking bolts, Sargent & Greenleaf mechanical dials, and biometric authentication. Can be faced with matching woodwork.

Life Support & Redundancy

Integrated positive-pressure HEPA air filtration system, concealed ballistic exhaust louvers, uninterruptible power supply (UPS), internal duress communication lines, and climate-controlled humidity regulation.
