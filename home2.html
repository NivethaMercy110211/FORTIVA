What Makes a Safe, A Safe?

A residential safe is defined by the thickness and grade of its steel body, the quality of its locking mechanism, the number and diameter of its locking bolts, and independent verification of its resistance to physical attack. Never assess a safe solely by weight or appearance.

Steel Body & Construction

Door and body steel thickness varies by grade. Higher-grade safes feature composite or concrete-filled walls that resist drilling, cutting and prying beyond basic steel plate.

Locking Bolts

High-grade safes deploy multiple hardened steel bolts on three or four sides of the door. Bolt diameter and hardening treatment are critical to attack resistance.

Locking Mechanism

Electronic keypads, mechanical combination dials and biometric units each offer different reliability, convenience and durability profiles. Independent certification matters here.

Fire-Resistant Liner

In fire-rated safes, insulating material between the outer steel and inner compartment absorbs heat. Paper and data ratings require different liner specifications.

Relocking Devices

Premium safes include internal relocking mechanisms that automatically engage if an attack is detected, blocking access even if the primary lock is compromised.

Certified Anchoring

Engineered fixing points secure the safe to a suitable floor or wall substrate, reducing removal risk while preserving the cabinet's tested structural integrity.

08 — Ownership

Care & Maintenance

A well-maintained safe performs reliably for decades. Here's what responsible ownership looks like.

Battery Maintenance

Electronic locks require fresh batteries annually or as indicated. Most units include an external emergency battery terminal. Never let batteries completely drain before replacement.

Mechanism Lubrication

Locking bolts and door hinges should be lubricated with manufacturer-specified products annually. Never use standard household oils which can attract dust and degrade lock components.

Regular Inspection

Inspect door seals, locking bolt engagement and interior condition periodically. After any fire event, have your safe professionally inspected before relying on it for continued use.
