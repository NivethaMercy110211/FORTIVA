Installation

Professional installation ensures your safe is securely positioned and correctly anchored. Our specialist team manages access, property protection, placement, and final setup with discretion.

White-Glove Protocol

The 6-Stage Engineering Installation Process

From initial structural calculation to final code programming, every step is executed by in-house background-checked specialists bound by strict non-disclosure agreements.

01
Confidential Survey

Structural Load & Route Assessment

Our lead engineer verifies floor joist ratings, underfloor heating loops using thermal imaging cameras, and precise doorway clearances. We analyze optimal room placement to maximize natural concealment while maintaining effortless daily access.

Surface Preservation

Architectural Surface & Floor Protection

Before moving a single kilogram, our riggers lay high-density interlocking neoprene floor armor over fine hardwoods, polished marble, and antique staircases. Specialized battery-powered rubber crawler systems disperse transit weight smoothly across floor surfaces.

Zero floor scuff guarantee Non-marking stair climbers
Floor & Surface Armor
04
Certified Anchoring

Chemical Resin Foundation Anchoring

We bore diamond-tipped precision pilot holes into the concrete substrate or install heavy steel backplates across floor joists. High-tensile Hilti structural chemical resin capsules are cured, creating an anchoring bond tested to withstand over 2.5 tonnes of upward extraction force.

Exceeds EN 1143-1 test specs Underwriter insurance certified

Structural Engineering

Anchor Technologies & Resistance

Insurance underwriters require that safes weighing under 1,000 kg be physically anchored to resist prying, levering, and physical removal attacks.

Foundation Bond

Chemical Resin Anchors

High-strength hybrid urethane methacrylate resin injected into diamond-cored concrete holes. Expands molecularly to bond steel M16/M20 threaded studs permanently to the building foundation.

Pull-Out Resistance: > 28 kN (2.8 Tonnes)
Joist Sandwich Plate

Joist Reinforcing Backplates

Engineered 10 mm laser-cut steel backing plates installed across multiple timber floor joists. High-grade through-bolts sandwich joist timber so the safe cannot be levered without breaking the home's primary framing.

Pull-Out Resistance: > 22 kN (2.2 Tonnes)
Masonry Expansion

Mechanical Expansion Shield

Heavy-gauge steel sleeve anchors expanding radially against dense masonry or brickwork walls. Ideal for secondary rear-wall anti-tilt stabilization in luxury master dressing rooms.

Shear Resistance: > 18 kN (1.8 Tonnes)
